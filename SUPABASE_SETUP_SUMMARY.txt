📊 WHAT WILL BE CREATED:
═════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

✅ 7 PostgreSQL Tables:
   • companies - Multi-tenant root collection
   • warehouses - Physical warehouse locations
   • products - Product catalog
   • inventory - Stock levels by product/warehouse
   • stock_movements - Immutable audit trail of changes
   • users - Application users linked to Supabase Auth
   • audit_logs - Immutable system audit trail

✅ Multi-Tenant Architecture:
   • Company-level data isolation
   • RLS (Row Level Security) enforced
   • Secure access boundaries

✅ PostgreSQL Features:
   • Foreign key relationships
   • Composite indexes for queries
   • Computed columns (e.g., available = quantity - reserved)
   • Constraints & validation
   • Stored procedures ready

✅ Security:
   • RLS policies on all tables
   • Role-based access (admin/manager/user)
   • Immutable audit trails
   • Company isolation

📝 COLLECTION OVERVIEW:
═════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

COMPANIES (Multi-tenant root):
  Fields: name, email, industry, website, country, active, subscription
  Purpose: Root collection for company segregation

WAREHOUSES (Physical locations):
  Fields: company_id, name, location, address, city, state, zip, capacity
  Relationships: Belongs to company

PRODUCTS (Product catalog):
  Fields: company_id, sku, name, description, category, price, cost, margin
  Relationships: Belongs to company

INVENTORY (Stock levels):
  Fields: company_id, product_id, warehouse_id, quantity, reserved, available
  Relationships: Belongs to company, product, warehouse
  Unique: (company_id, product_id, warehouse_id)

STOCK_MOVEMENTS (Audit trail - IMMUTABLE):
  Fields: company_id, inventory_id, type, quantity_before/after, reason
  Relationships: Belongs to company, inventory, product, warehouse
  Note: Can't be updated or deleted

USERS (Application users):
  Fields: company_id, email, name, role, active, permissions
  Relationships: Linked to Supabase Auth, belongs to company

AUDIT_LOGS (System logs - IMMUTABLE):
  Fields: company_id, user_id, action, resource, resource_id, changes
  Relationships: Belongs to company
  Note: Can't be updated or deleted
